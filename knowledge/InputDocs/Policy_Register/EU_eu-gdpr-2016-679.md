## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Art. 9(1)-(2)(a) [verified]
Prohibits processing health data and identifying biometric data unless an exception such as explicit consent applies.
Relevance: HRV/EDA-derived attention or stress states in an ADHD context are very likely health data; explicit granular consent needed. Note Art. 9(1) covers biometric data only when processed for uniquely identifying a person.

### Art. 8 [verified]
Child consent for online services valid from 16 (Member States may lower to 13); below that parental authorisation is required.
Relevance: Younger students need verifiable parental consent.

### Arts. 22, 35 [verified]
Limits solely automated decisions with significant effects; DPIA required for high-risk processing such as large-scale special-category data.
Relevance: DPIA effectively mandatory.
