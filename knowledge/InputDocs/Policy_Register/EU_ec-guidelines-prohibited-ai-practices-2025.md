## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Art. 5(1)(f) examples [verified]
An education institution using emotion recognition to infer students' interest and attention is a prohibited example; fatigue detection for pilots/drivers is not emotion recognition.
Relevance: Directly maps onto attention-state monitoring; avoid institutional deployment.

### Intentions; biometric limitation [UNVERIFIED]
Covers intention inference too, but only biometric-based inference; text sentiment is outside.
Relevance: Gaze and keystroke dynamics are biometric-derived; typed content sentiment may not be.
