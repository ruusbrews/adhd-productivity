## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Art. 5(3); Arts. 8-9 [UNVERIFIED]
Practices affecting groups vulnerable through mental or physical infirmity, age or credulity are judged from that group's view; aggressive practices incl. undue influence are unfair.
Relevance: Upsells timed to low-attention or stress states risk being unfair.
