## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### General wellness product definition [verified]
Low-risk (non-invasive, non-implanted) products intended only for general wellness fall under enforcement discretion; issued 6 Jan 2026, superseding 2019.
Relevance: Non-invasive HRV/EDA/motion wearables with focus/stress wellness claims can stay outside device oversight.

### Disease-related claims [UNVERIFIED]
Claims to diagnose or treat a specific disease fall outside the policy.
Relevance: 'ADHD management' claims probably exceed it; not verified clause-by-clause.
