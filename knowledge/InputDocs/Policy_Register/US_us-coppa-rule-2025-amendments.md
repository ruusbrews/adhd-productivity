## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### §312.2 'personal information' (10) [UNVERIFIED]
Adds biometric identifiers usable for automated recognition, e.g. fingerprints, iris/retina, voiceprints, gait patterns, facial templates, genetic data.
Relevance: Gait/motion and face templates from under-13s covered; HRV/EDA not named.

### §312.5(a)(2); retention policy [UNVERIFIED]
Separate parental consent before disclosing children's data to third parties; written retention policy; compliance due 22 April 2026.
Relevance: No ad/analytics SDK sharing without separate consent; define deletion schedules.
