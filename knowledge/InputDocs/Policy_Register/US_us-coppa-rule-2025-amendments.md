## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### §312.2 'personal information' (10) [verified]
Adds biometric identifiers usable for automated recognition, e.g. fingerprints, iris/retina, voiceprints, gait patterns, facial templates, genetic data.
Relevance: Gait/motion and face templates from under-13s covered; HRV/EDA not named. COPPA covers children under 13 only (§312.2 'Child'), so a 13-17-year-old user is outside it.

### §312.5(a)(2); §312.10; §312.2 'support for the internal operations' (2) [verified]
Parents must be able to consent to collection and use without consenting to disclosure to third parties, unless disclosure is integral to the service, and the operator must obtain separate verifiable parental consent to such disclosure (§312.5(a)(2)); data may not be retained indefinitely and a written retention policy must state purposes, business need and a deletion timeframe (§312.10); data collected for internal operations may not be used for behavioral advertising or to amass a profile on a specific individual (§312.2). Compliance date 22 April 2026 as reported, not shown in eCFR.
Relevance: No ad/analytics SDK sharing without separate consent; inferred-state profiles must not feed behavioral ads; define deletion schedules. Applies to under-13s only.
