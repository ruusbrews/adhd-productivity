## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Art. 2 (exclusions) [verified]
Excludes health personal data governed by its own legislation and free zones with their own data laws.
Relevance: If treated as health data, UAE health-data law may apply instead; DIFC/ADGM hosting changes regime.

### Art. 28 [verified]
Cabinet to issue Executive Regulations within six months.
Relevance: 2026 secondary sources (Chambers 2026, uaeexperthub) say still unissued, so breach timelines, DPO thresholds and fines remain unclear.

### Sensitive data and consent provisions [UNVERIFIED]
Sensitive data includes health, biometric and genetic data; processing generally needs consent subject to exceptions.
Relevance: Treat physiological/attention data as sensitive.
