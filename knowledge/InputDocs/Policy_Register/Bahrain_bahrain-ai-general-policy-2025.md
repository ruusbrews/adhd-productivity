## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Section 3 Scope [verified]
Applies to all government entities in Bahrain.
Relevance: Binds only public-sector deployments (e.g., public schools).

### Section 4, First and Second Principles [verified]
Final decisions on important matters stay under human control; AI must prevent physical or psychological harm.
Relevance: Human-in-the-loop for escalations; psychological-harm testing.

### Glossary 'Sensitive Data'; Section 2 [verified]
Sensitive data includes health information; entities must comply with the PDPL and GCC AI Ethics guideline.
Relevance: Physiological data in government use is sensitive.
