## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Art. 16 [verified]
Data on ethnic origin, children, health, physical or psychological condition, religion, marital relations and criminal offences is 'special nature' data, processable only with permission from the competent department.
Relevance: Attention/stress/ADHD data and any child data need an NCSA permit in addition to consent.

### Art. 17 [verified]
Child-directed website operators must give notice, obtain explicit guardian consent and not condition participation on unnecessary data.
Relevance: Student versions need guardian consent and minimisation.
