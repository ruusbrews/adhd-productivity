## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Standard 13 (Nudge techniques) [verified]
No nudges leading children to give unnecessary data or weaken privacy protections.
Relevance: Rewards must never unlock extra sensor permissions or sharing.

### Standards 1, 2, 5, 7, 12 [verified]
Best interests of the child, DPIA, no detrimental data use, high-privacy defaults, profiling off by default.
Relevance: Attention profiling of under-18s off by default.

### Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (reported) [UNVERIFIED]
A secondary source says children's protection was embedded in UK GDPR Art. 25(1) from 19 June 2025.
Relevance: Would make child-centred design statutory; NOT verified on legislation.gov.uk.
