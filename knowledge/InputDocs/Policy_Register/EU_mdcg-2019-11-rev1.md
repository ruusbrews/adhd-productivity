## Provisions relevant to a digital ADHD-support app (paraphrased)

### Qualification [UNVERIFIED]
Software is medical device software based on intended purpose (e.g., diagnosis, monitoring, treatment or alleviation of disease or disability), regardless of platform.
Relevance: 'Helps manage ADHD' claims can make the app MDSW; 'study habits' claims less likely.

### MDR Annex VIII Rule 11 (as explained) [UNVERIFIED]
Software informing diagnostic/therapeutic decisions is at least Class IIa, other MDSW generally Class I; rev.1 (June 2025) clarified intended purpose and modules.
Relevance: Modular design can keep wellness modules outside MDR.

### MDCG 2025-6 (listed on page) [verified]
June 2025 FAQ on interplay between MDR/IVDR and the AI Act.
Relevance: Notified-body MDSW makes its AI high-risk via AI Act Art. 6(1), now from 2 Aug 2028.
